I’m Keyur Kadia. I started my career in civil engineering, but my interests gradually moved toward data, technology and business systems.

Today I work as a Data Analyst with business data, ERP systems, SQL, Python, Power BI and data modelling. Working with operational and manufacturing data changed how I think about analytics.

> A dashboard is only the final layer.

Behind it are business processes, databases, data models, pipelines, data quality — and the systems that generate the data in the first place. That’s why I’m moving deeper into analytics engineering and data engineering.

Datavique is where I document that journey through projects, experiments, research and technical writing.
